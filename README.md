# 🔥 Blaze Flow

**Blaze Flow** is an all-in-one creative agency management platform designed to bring projects, clients, video reviews, tasks, files, team collaboration, billing, and workflows into one centralized workspace.

## ✨ Core Features

* **Workspace Management** — Multi-tenant workspaces for managing agency operations.
* **Projects** — Organize projects, members, files, tasks, and activity.
* **Client Management** — Manage clients and project-level client access.
* **Video Review & Approval** — Versioned video uploads, timestamped comments, annotations, review stages, and approvals.
* **Task Management** — Track project tasks and team responsibilities.
* **File Management** — Organize client documents and creative assets.
* **Team & Access Control** — Workspace/project roles and permission-based access.
* **Calendar** — Centralized project and team scheduling.
* **Billing** — Manage agency billing workflows.
* **Reports** — Track operational and project insights.
* **AI Assistant** — AI-assisted workflows and productivity features.

## 🎯 Goal

Creative agencies often depend on multiple disconnected tools for project management, client communication, creative review, file sharing, and business operations.

Blaze Flow aims to reduce this **tool sprawl** by providing a unified platform where agencies can manage their workflow from project creation to client approval.

## 🏗️ Architecture

Blaze Flow follows a **workspace-based multi-tenant architecture** with structured access control for:

* Internal team members
* Client members
* Project-specific members
* Guest reviewers

The platform is designed with modularity and scalability in mind so individual business capabilities can evolve independently.
